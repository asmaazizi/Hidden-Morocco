Marrakech

Escape Marrakech for an exciting adventure in the Agafay Desert. This experience combines action, beautiful landscapes, and authentic Moroccan culture in one unforgettable evening. Your journey begins with hotel pickup and transfer to the desert camp. After a short introduction and safety briefing, you’ll meet your guide and wear traditional nomadic clothing before enjoying a peaceful camel ride across the desert. Then, feel the thrill of driving a quad bike through the rocky hills and open spaces of Agafay. Stop at a scenic spot to admire the stunning sunset as the desert changes colors in the evening light. After the adventure, relax under a traditional Berber tent and enjoy a delicious Moroccan dinner. As you dine, take in the calm atmosphere of the desert and admire the clear, star-filled sky.

Ouarzazate

Discover the magic of Morocco on this 3-day desert journey from Ouarzazate to Marrakech. Travel through dramatic landscapes, ancient kasbahs, lush valleys, and the golden dunes of the Sahara. Enjoy a camel ride at sunset, try sandboarding on soft desert sands, and spend a memorable night in a traditional desert camp under a sky full of stars. This experience blends adventure, culture, and authentic Moroccan hospitality for an unforgettable escape.

Detailed Itinerary

1

Day 1: Ouarzazate – Ait Ben Haddou – Dades Valley

  • Your trip begins in Ouarzazate with a visit to the legendary Ait Ben Haddou Kasbah, a fortified village famous for its historical architecture and appearances in international films and series.
  • After exploring the kasbah and enjoying lunch nearby, continue through beautiful desert landscapes and traditional Berber villages along the Road of the Thousand Kasbahs.
  • Arrive in the Dades Valley in the evening, where you will check in to a comfortable hotel or riad. Dinner and overnight stay included.
2

Day 2: Dades Valley – Todra Gorges – Merzouga Desert

  • After breakfast, head toward the spectacular Todra Gorges. Take time to walk between the dramatic cliffs rising up to 300 meters high.
  • Later, continue to Merzouga where your camel guide will be waiting. Ride your camel across the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi while enjoying a breathtaking sunset.
  • Arrive at your desert camp where you’ll enjoy dinner, traditional Berber music, and a peaceful night beneath the stars.
3

Day 3: Merzouga – Ouarzazate – Marrakech

  • Wake up early to admire the sunrise over the dunes. After breakfast at the camp, begin the return journey through ever-changing desert and mountain scenery.
  • Pass through Ouarzazate and continue toward Marrakech. Arrival in the evening, marking the end of your unforgettable Sahara adventure.

Morocco’s Grand Contrast

Experience the absolute best of Morocco on this epic 4-day journey that connects the red city of Marrakech to the blue pearl of Chefchaouen. This isn't just a transfer; it's a deep dive into the heart of the kingdom. Traverse the high peaks of the Atlas, ride through the golden dunes of the Sahara, and witness the ancient history of Fes before ending in the tranquility of the Rif Mountains.

1

Day 1: Marrakech › Ait Benhaddou › Ouarzazate › Valley of Roses

  • Depart from Marrakech at 8:00 AM, ascending the winding roads of the Tizi n'Tichka pass (2,260m) in the High Atlas Mountains.
  • Marvel at the Berber villages clinging to the hillsides before arriving at the world-famous Kasbah Ait Benhaddou.
  • After a guided tour and lunch, we continue through Ouarzazate—the "Hollywood of Africa"—and follow the legendary "Road of a Thousand Kasbahs."
  • We'll conclude our first day in the fragrant Valley of Roses. (Dinner included)
2

Day 2: Valley of Roses › Todra Gorges › Merzouga (Sahara)

  • After breakfast, head to the Dades Valley to admire the unique "Monkey Toes" rock formations.
  • Move toward the Todra Gorges, where you’ll walk beneath 300-meter-high limestone cliffs—a true natural wonder.
  • After lunch in Rissani, arrive in Merzouga, where your camel caravan awaits.
  • Trek into the heart of the Erg Chebbi dunes to witness a spectacular desert sunset. The night is spent in a traditional camp, filled with Moroccan hospitality, music, and a sky full of stars. (Breakfast, Dinner)
3

Day 3: Merzouga › Ziz Valley › Azrou › Ifrane › Fes

  • Wake up early for a sunrise that will stay with you forever.
  • After trekking back to Merzouga and enjoying a hot shower and breakfast, begin the drive north.
  • Pass through the lush Ziz Valley and the Middle Atlas Mountains. Stop in Azrou to visit the ancient cedar forests and meet the local monkeys, followed by a coffee break in the alpine town of Ifrane.
  • By early evening, arrive in the imperial city of Fes for your overnight stay. (Breakfast, Dinner)
4

Day 4: Fes Medina Exploration › Chefchaouen

  • Spend your final morning diving into the rich history of Fes.
  • With your guide (optional), explore the ancient tanneries, mosques, and artisan workshops of the world’s oldest medina.
  • After a traditional lunch in the heart of the old city, head north into the Rif Mountains.
  • The tour concludes with your arrival in the mesmerizing "Blue City" of Chefchaouen, where we’ll drop you off at your accommodation. (Breakfast)

Essaouira

Formerly Mogador, Essaouira is a fortified port city with bohemian charm, swept by the Atlantic winds. Stroll through its UNESCO World Heritage medina, admire its historic ramparts, and watch the ballet of blue fishing boats in the harbor. Renowned for its music festivals, art galleries, and water sports, Essaouira offers a refreshing cultural and seaside getaway.

Fès

Spiritual and cultural capital of Morocco, Fes is home to the largest and oldest pedestrian medina in the world, Fes el-Bali. The Grand Moroccan Traverse

Transform your transit between Morocco's most iconic imperial cities into the adventure of a lifetime. Instead of a simple transfer, embark on a captivating 4-day overland journey from Fes to Marrakech, taking you deep into the heart of the Sahara Desert.

1

Day 1: Fes › Ifrane › Azrou › Midelt › Erfoud › Merzouga

  • Your expedition begins with an 8:00 AM pickup from your Fes accommodation. We head south into the Middle Atlas Mountains.
  • First stop in the picturesque, alpine-style town of Ifrane, the "Switzerland of Morocco."
  • Dive into the lush cedar forests of Azrou to greet the friendly local Barbary macaques.
  • After descending through Midelt and enjoying lunch, cruise along the spectacular Ziz Valley, a lush ribbon of palm groves and ancient kasbahs.
  • By late afternoon, arrive in Merzouga. Check into your hotel for a relaxing dinner and overnight stay.
2

Day 2: Merzouga › Rissani › Khamlia › Erg Chebbi Dunes

  • After breakfast, explore the magic of the Saharan region. Visit the historic city of Rissani, the birthplace of the ruling Alawite dynasty, known for its traditional markets and beautiful architectural monuments.
  • Visit the village of Khamlia to experience the hypnotic, rhythmic sounds of traditional Gnaoua music.
  • As late afternoon approaches, meet your camel caravan. Embark on a scenic ride across the golden waves of Erg Chebbi as the sun sets.
  • Arrive at your nomad desert camp. Enjoy a magical night of Berber drumming, a hearty dinner, and stargazing in the pristine desert silence.
3

Day 3: Erg Chebbi › Merzouga › Todra Gorges › Dades Valley

  • Wake up early to witness a majestic desert sunrise painting the dunes in vibrant colors—a true highlight of the trip.
  • After trekking back to the Merzouga edge on your camel, depart toward Erfoud and the dramatic landscapes of the southeast.
  • Visit the spectacular Todra Gorges, where you can take a peaceful walk beside the river, flanked by massive 600-foot canyon walls.
  • Continue to the Dades Valley, passing the fascinating rock formations known as the "Monkey Fingers" (Tamnalt Hills). Overnight in the heart of the Dades Gorges, complete with dinner and breakfast.
4

Day 4: Dades Valley › Skoura › Ouarzazate › Ait Benhaddou › Marrakech

  • Drive through the fragrant Valley of the Roses and the palm-rich Skoura Oasis before reaching Ouarzazate, the "Gateway to the Desert."
  • Explore the magnificent Kasbah Ait Benhaddou, a UNESCO World Heritage site famous for Hollywood blockbusters like Gladiator and Lawrence of Arabia.
  • After enjoying lunch near the Kasbah, begin the scenic ascent over the High Atlas Mountains via the famous Tizi N'Tichka pass.
  • Enjoy sweeping views before descending into the vibrant city of Marrakech, dropping you off safely at your Riad or Hotel.

The Blue Pearl Escape

A tranquil, private 2-day getaway from Casablanca to the enchanting Blue City of Chefchaouen, nestled high in the Rif Mountains. A perfect blend of serene vibes, rich history, and captivating local culture.

1

Day 1: Casablanca to Chefchaouen (Discovering the Blue Medina)

  • Your journey begins with a morning pick-up from Casablanca. Settle into your comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle as we drive north toward the breathtaking Rif Mountains.
  • Upon arriving in Chefchaouen, you'll immediately see why its original Berber name means "horns," referencing the twin mountain peaks towering over the town.
  • Spend your day wandering the vibrant medina. Let your senses guide you as you take in the aroma of freshly baked bread and sizzling tagines.
  • Visit the historic Kasbah, the Grand Mosque, and take advantage of the endless photography opportunities.
  • After a full day of exploration, relax and spend a peaceful night in a traditional, highly-rated Riad in the heart of the Blue City. (Dinner included)
2

Day 2: Chefchaouen to Casablanca (Morning Explorations & Return)

  • Wake up to a wonderful, traditional Moroccan breakfast at your Riad.
  • Take advantage of your remaining free time to soak up the relaxed atmosphere, snap a few final photos of the colorful streets, or pick up some last-minute souvenirs.
  • In the afternoon, meet your driver for a comfortable and scenic return journey.
  • Arriving back in Casablanca by the early evening (~06:30 PM) to conclude your tour. (Breakfast included)
